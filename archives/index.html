Hyperlink

... was an innocent little Neocities project with a most natural conclusion, as well as an accidental commentary on human nature by virtue of its eventual cessation. The idea was simple: everyone gets their own directory on the website, as well as full unfettered access to the Neocities account that the website is bound to. What could go wrong? Well ... haha. Anyway, here's the little place I used to have on it. I always felt like I nailed the vibes with this one. Shame that it was so short-lived! I even wrote some chill music for it! Ah, good times and good memories.

The website link above places you at the last snapshot of the website before the incidents began to unfold. Clicking the rightward arrow on the Internet Archive toolbar a few times may prove to be quite entertaining!
