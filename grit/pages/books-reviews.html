大今 良時

不滅のあなたへ (1~4)

★ ★ ★ ★ ☆ 🧍

Very interesting story about something learning how to be various things and beings (e.g., a rock, a wolf, a man), as the world—according to some edgy godlike figure, that is—needs to be "stored" (保存) onto it before falling apart.

Volumes one through three follow the thing in three different situations, and in completely different settings, giving the impression that this will be some kind of nomadic soul- or meaning-searching sort of adventure about the vicissitudes of life ... until the fourth one, that is, which thus far unconventionally continues the third, with the thing choosing to continue living a strictly human life, and then after some intense conflict just being dumped onto the prison-island of despair and agony at the end, where the prisoners (slaves) kill each other for sport and whatnot. Not only that, but according to the preview of the next volume, his unlawful captor somehow does not understand why he (it) wouldn't like (好き) her—I guess after all she's done to make it up to him – preposterous!

This complete tonal shift—although I do understand where it comes from—has kind of killed my enthusiasm to read any further, so I will let this work sit for a while until I decide whether I want to see the story through to the end or not.
